About the studio

Blackcat Forge is an independent game-asset studio based in Louisiana. We make the sounds and skies that make a game world feel real — mechanical sound design for robots and machines, deep-space environments, and clean interface audio — built by hand, checked piece by piece, and made to drop straight into a project.

Every pack is held to one standard: if it doesn't sound and look like it belongs in a shipped game, it doesn't leave the forge.

What we make

⚙

Mech & Machine Sound Packs

Sci-fi machinery, garage ambience, servos, hydraulics, and combat effects — recorded, layered, and mastered for game engines.

✦

HDR Skyboxes & Space Art

Deep-space nebulae, planet horizons, eclipse skies, and hangar interiors in high-resolution HDR, ready for skyboxes and environments.

♬

UI & Interface Audio

Blips, confirmations, HUD sweeps, and retro interface sounds that make menus feel responsive and alive.

The standard

Presentation is everything. Each release is solver-checked, ear-checked, and eye-checked before it ships — clean files, consistent naming, honest previews, and no filler. Developers trusting our work with their games is the whole business, so the work has to deserve that trust.
